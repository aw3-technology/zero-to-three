## Book at a Glance

*Zero to Three* argues that building successful ventures in Web3 and AI requires progressing through four stages, each demanding different skills and mindsets:

| Stage | Core Question | What You Build | Key Chapters |
|-------|--------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Zero** | "What do I uniquely see?" | Founder mindset, resilience, asymmetric insight | 11-18 |
| **One** | "Does this work and create value?" | Working product, protocol, or service | 19-31 |
| **Two** | "Do people trust this enough to depend on it?" | Community, network effects, governance | 32-35 |
| **Three** | "Can this thrive without me?" | Platform, standards, ecosystem stewardship | 36-39 |

**Part I** (Chapters 01-10, 42) sets the foundation: the technological, economic, and global context every builder needs. **Part VI** (Chapters 40-41) looks beyond, at exponential impact and cross-industry transformation.

**The framework is recursive, not linear.** Founders cycle through these stages multiple times. A Chapter 36 insight may send you back to Chapter 13.

## Book Description

*Zero to Three* is the definitive guide for building successful ventures in the age of Web3 and AI. Inspired by Peter Thiel's "Zero to One," this book addresses the unique challenges and opportunities of decentralized technology and artificial intelligence.

The book presents a comprehensive framework for entrepreneurship that acknowledges how Web3 and AI represent fundamentally new technological paradigms requiring different approaches than traditional Silicon Valley startup methodologies.

## Preface

This project is intentionally living: it updates as the landscape shifts. Contributions, critiques, and case studies are welcome, and governance follows open collaboration principles. Treat this as a practical playbook -- use the checklists and exercises.

## Prologue

Why this book and why now: after successes and failures across AI and Web3, the most durable pattern was a shift from product-centric thinking to system stewardship. The journey from Zero to Three is recursive -- this book helps you know where you are and what to do next.

---

## Who This Book Is For

**Primary audience: Founders and builders** creating ventures at the intersection of Web3 and AI. Every framework, checklist, and case study is designed for people actively building -- whether you are a solo founder shipping your first protocol, a technical co-founder choosing your stack, or a repeat entrepreneur entering the Web3/AI space for the first time.

**Secondary audiences** will also find value here, though the lens is always entrepreneurial:

- **Investors** evaluating Web3/AI ventures -- use the frameworks to sharpen due diligence (start with Part I and the Case Studies Compendium)
- **Product and technical leaders** at crypto/AI companies -- use Parts III-IV for systems thinking and scaling playbooks
- **Policy and legal professionals** -- see the governance chapters (Ch. 28, 34) and Global Perspectives (Ch. 07) for regulatory context
- **Students and researchers** -- treat the book as a survey with primary sources; the bibliography and Further Reading sections provide jumping-off points

> If you are not building something, you will still learn -- but this book will constantly ask you to *do* things. That is by design.
